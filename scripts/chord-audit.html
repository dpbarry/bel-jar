BelJar chord audit

This measures what reserved-chords.mjs currently asserts from documentation. Press every chord below, in any order. A chord that reaches this page turns green; whatever is still grey at the end is one the browser took before the page ever saw it.

Some of these open or close a browser tab on purpose — that is the finding. Results are saved after every keypress, so losing this tab loses nothing: just reopen the file. If a chord turned green and the browser also did its own thing, click that row to flag it.
